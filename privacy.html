Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Zenyth may handle information when you use the bot on Discord.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the potential collection, use, storage, and disclosure of information by Zenyth (“the Bot”). The actual practices depend on the features enabled and the bot’s implementation.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Zenyth may process:

  • Discord user IDs to identify users for commands, permissions, or moderation.
  • Server IDs to identify servers where the bot is installed.
  • Channel IDs, message content, and command inputs when needed to respond to commands or provide enabled features.
  • Configuration data, such as role IDs, channel IDs, preferences, and server settings.
  • Moderation records, such as warnings or moderation actions, if those features are enabled.
  • Diagnostic logs, such as errors and technical events used to maintain reliability and security.

This list describes possible data categories, not a claim that every category is collected. The operator should update this page to match the bot’s actual code and configuration.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to provide bot features, process commands, remember server settings, perform requested moderation actions, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, protect service security, and respond to support or legal requests.

3. Message Content

Some commands or features may process message content or command arguments. Whether this information is logged or stored depends on the feature implementation. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive credentials to the bot.

4. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in databases, configuration files, or logs where required to operate the bot. The operator should retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose and applicable legal requirements, then delete or anonymize it when appropriate.

5. Sharing of Information

Zenyth does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting, database, or monitoring providers that support the service, or disclosed when reasonably necessary to protect users and the service or comply with applicable law.

6. Discord and Third-Party Services

Zenyth operates through Discord and may rely on third-party infrastructure. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices. Read the Discord Privacy Policy.

7. Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Requests and Deletion

To ask about information associated with your use of Zenyth or request deletion, contact the operator using the support details below. Please provide enough information to locate relevant records, but never send passwords or authentication tokens. Some information may need to be retained where required or permitted by law.

9. Children's Privacy

Zenyth must not be used in a way that violates applicable age requirements or child-protection laws. If you believe information has been collected unlawfully, contact the operator so the concern can be reviewed.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the bot’s features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with a revised date.

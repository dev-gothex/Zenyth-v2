Zenyth — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zenyth (“the Bot”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Bot on Discord.

By using Zenyth, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Zenyth may process the following information:

- Discord User IDs: To identify users when executing commands or managing permissions.
- Discord Server IDs: To identify servers where Zenyth is installed.
- Channel and Message Information: To perform requested commands, moderation actions, or other enabled features.
- Server Configuration: Settings, custom prefixes, role IDs, channel IDs, and other configuration data.
- Moderation Records: Information about warnings, timeouts, bans, or other moderation actions when these features are enabled.
- Command Inputs: Information submitted when interacting with the Bot.
- Technical Logs: Error reports and diagnostic information needed to maintain reliability and security.

Zenyth does not necessarily collect every category listed above. Actual collection depends on the features implemented and enabled.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Provide and maintain Zenyth's features.
- Process commands and user requests.
- Apply server configurations and permissions.
- Perform moderation and administrative actions.
- Diagnose errors and improve reliability.
- Prevent spam, abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
- Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. Message Content

Some features may temporarily process message content or command arguments to perform their intended functions.

Message content is not necessarily stored permanently. Whether content is logged or retained depends on the implemented features and configuration.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, authentication tokens, financial details, or other sensitive information with the Bot.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in databases, configuration files, or logs required to operate Zenyth.

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its stated purpose, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the feature involved. Where applicable, expired or unnecessary information will be deleted or anonymized.

5. Data Sharing

Zenyth does not sell user information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

- Operate the Bot using hosting providers or infrastructure services.
- Protect the Bot, its users, or its infrastructure against abuse.
- Comply with valid legal obligations.
- Respond to security incidents or enforce applicable terms.

Service providers may process information on behalf of the Bot's operators where necessary to provide their services.

6. Discord and Third-Party Services

Zenyth operates through Discord and may depend on third-party hosting, database, or monitoring services.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own privacy practices and terms. Third-party services may have separate privacy policies.

Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy
